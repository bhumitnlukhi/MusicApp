01 Information We Do Not Collect

Pulse does not operate user accounts, cloud databases, or tracking infrastructure. We do not collect, monetize, or transmit:

  • Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Financial Information: The application is entirely free. We do not process transactions, credit cards, or banking information.
  • Location Information: We never request access to GPS, cellular location, or network location.
  • Sensory Data: We never access your device microphone, camera, or sensors.
  • Device Records: We do not read device contacts, photos, SMS, or file directories outside the app's private sandbox.

02 Information Handled Locally On Your Device

All your personalization features operate strictly on-device without cloud synchronization:

User Playlists & Liked Songs

Custom playlists, songs you mark as liked, and your recently played history are stored locally via SQLite and SharedPreferences. This information is accessible only to Pulse and is never uploaded to any remote server.

Cached Artwork & Audio Buffers

Album cover artwork and media chunks are temporarily cached in your app storage to provide instant playback and conserve cellular bandwidth. You can clear this cache at any time via your device's application manager.

Data Deletion: Because your data resides entirely on your device, uninstalling Pulse or choosing "Clear Data" in Android settings permanently deletes 100% of your saved library.

03 Network Requests & Content Providers

To discover music, fetch lyrics, and stream audio, Pulse makes secure HTTPS requests to third-party services:

  • Media Streaming: Queries made in the search bar or library are transmitted over encrypted TLS connections to retrieve track information, album art, and audio streams. These requests convey standard network parameters (IP address, User-Agent) necessary for internet communication, but no user identifiers.
  • Synchronized Lyrics (LRCLIB): Pulse retrieves synced lyrics from LRCLIB, an open community database. Requests include only song title, artist name, and duration.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: Pulse contains no ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics SDKs.

04 Device Permissions

Pulse requests only the essential system permissions required to provide continuous background audio playback, adhering strictly to Google Play's Least Privilege standards:

Android Permission Purpose & Justification
INTERNET Enables streaming audio playback, downloading album artwork, and retrieving song lyrics.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Required on Android 14+ (API 34+) to prevent the operating system from terminating audio when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents device hardware from entering deep sleep while decoding media during playback.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Enables the playback notification with media controls (Play/Pause, Next, Previous) in the notification tray and lock screen on Android 13+ (API 33+).

05 Content Aggregation & DMCA

Pulse functions strictly as an audio player client and indexing interface. Pulse does not own, host, produce, or upload media files to any remote server. All media streams and album covers are delivered from publicly available content networks.

DMCA & Copyright Takedowns

We respect intellectual property rights. If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and believe content indexed through Pulse infringes on your copyright, please submit a formal notification with the specific track/artist details to our inquiry email. We respond promptly to all legitimate inquiries.

06 Data Security

Because Pulse does not maintain external user accounts or remote personal databases, your listening data is immune to cloud data breaches. All internet communications use standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption.

07 Children's Privacy

Pulse is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is collected from any user, children's privacy is preserved by design under COPPA and GDPR.
