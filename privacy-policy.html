01

Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many modern media apps, Pulse does not create user profiles, sell user data, or monitor personal activity. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, address, or social login credentials.
  • Financial Information: The app is free to use. We do not process credit cards, bank details, or in-app payment information.
  • Location Data: We never access your device GPS, coarse location, or Wi-Fi network location.
  • Biometrics & Hardware Recording: We do not access your microphone, camera, or biometric sensors.
  • Contacts & Photos: We do not read your device contact list, photo library, or SMS messages.
02

Information Handled Locally On Your Device

Pulse saves your custom preferences and activity strictly within your device's isolated application sandbox:

User Playlists & Favorites

Custom playlists created by you, your "Liked Songs" list, and your recently played song history are saved locally using SQLite and SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

Temporary Artwork & Audio Cache

Album cover artwork and media chunks are temporarily cached on your local storage to enable smooth transitions, offline responsiveness, and avoid unnecessary re-downloads. You can clear this cache anytime from your device's App Settings.

Your Data, Your Control: Because your data resides solely on your phone or tablet, clearing app storage or uninstalling Pulse immediately purges 100% of your saved playlists and preferences.

03

Network Requests & Content Providers

To discover, stream audio, and display lyrics, Pulse connects via encrypted HTTPS requests to public external services:

  • Media Streaming & Metadata: When you search or play songs, queries are transmitted over HTTPS to content servers to fetch track metadata (titles, artist names, album art) and stream audio streams. These requests contain standard HTTP headers (such as User-Agent and device IP) required for internet routing, but no user-identifying profiles.
  • Lyrics Provider (LRCLIB): Pulse retrieves synchronized and plain-text lyrics from LRCLIB, an open community database. Requests include song title, artist name, and duration to match lyrics. No personal data is sent.
  • No Third-Party Ad Networks: We do not integrate programmatic advertising SDKs (e.g. AdMob, Unity Ads) that track users across other apps or websites.
04

Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

Pulse requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to deliver a fluid music listening experience, complying with Google Play's Least Privilege principles:

Android Permission Usage & Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Enables the app to stream audio, fetch track metadata, retrieve cover art, and load song lyrics.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Required on Android 14+ (API 34+) to keep audio playing uninterrupted when you minimize the app, switch to another application, or lock your device.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Prevents your phone's processor from going to sleep while actively decoding and playing audio tracks.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Allows Pulse to display media controls (Play/Pause, Skip, Previous, Track Title) in your notification shade and on your lock screen on Android 13+ (API 33+).
05

Intellectual Property, Content Aggregation & DMCA

Content Disclaimer: Pulse is a third-party audio player and indexing interface. Pulse does not own, produce, host, store, or upload media files to any servers. All audio streams and album artwork are parsed and delivered from publicly accessible third-party networks and CDNs.

Notice and Takedown Procedure (DMCA)

We respect intellectual property rights. If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent and believe that content accessible through Pulse infringes upon your copyright, please submit a formal takedown request with the specific track/artist details to our designated contact email below. We act expeditiously to remove or block access to infringing links.

06

Data Security

Because we do not store your personal records or library on cloud databases, your data is inherently protected from external server data breaches. All network communications for audio streaming and artwork retrieval are conducted over industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

07

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Pulse is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because our app collects zero personal data, parents and guardians can be confident that children's privacy is preserved.
